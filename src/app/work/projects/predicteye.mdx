## Overview

Predictye is a free tool for finding out what your things are worth, now and later. Upload a photo of an item, add a few details, and get a value range today and a projected range for a future year. Estimates are shown as ranges and are not guarantees.

It started as a product forecasting platform for physical goods. It has since grown into five tools:

- **Predictye Value** values an item you own, now and in the future.
- **Predictye Property** shows what a home could be worth later.
- **Predictye Rent** compares renting against buying.
- **Buy smart** checks whether a price is fair before you buy.
- **Predictye Plan** helps you find the best time to sell or hold.

It covers categories from electronics and furniture to watches, vehicles, and collectibles, and it needs no signup.
